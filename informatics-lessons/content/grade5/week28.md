# Grade 5 - Week 28: Digital media
## Learning Objectives
- Explain Digital media in clear, age-appropriate language.
- Identify the key parts or steps of Digital media.
- Apply Digital media to a real-life or classroom example.
- Demonstrate: Characterize digital media.
- Use Digital media vocabulary accurately in short explanations.

## Assessment Criteria
- Uses correct terminology when explaining Digital media.
- Completes the practice activity accurately.
- Responds to oral questions with clear reasoning.
- Shows understanding of: characterize digital media.

## Key Vocabulary
- digital: a key term related to Digital media
- media: a key term related to Digital media
- characterize: a key term related to Digital media
- digital: a key term related to Digital media
- media: a key term related to Digital media
- input: data entered into a system
- output: results produced by a system
- process: steps that transform input to output
- system: connected parts working together
- accuracy: being correct or free of errors

## Lesson Timeline
- Introduction - 5 minutes
- Main Activity - 25 minutes
- Wrap-up - 10 minutes

## Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introduce Digital media and link it to everyday technology.
- Warm-up questions:
- - Where do we encounter this idea in daily life?
- - How does this help computers work correctly?
- Real-life examples:
- - A classroom example connected to Digital media.
- - Link to: characterize digital media.
- Teacher: Briefly model the key idea using a quick sketch or object.
- Students: Share one example they know and explain why it fits.

## Main Content (25 minutes)
- Define Digital media in clear terms.
- Break down the key components or steps.
- Show a short, concrete example.
- Connect to prior knowledge from earlier lessons.
- Focus point: Characterize digital media.
- Teacher: Model the process step-by-step and check for understanding.
- Students: Take brief notes and answer a quick concept check.

### Detailed Topic Study
- **Goal (2 min):** Define digital media and give examples.
- **Key ideas (8 min):** Digital media includes text, images, audio, and video stored as digital data. Examples: websites, e-books, music files.
- **Guided demo (6 min):** Sort classroom examples into digital or non-digital media.
- **Hands-on practice (6 min):** Students create a simple digital media poster (text + image).
- **Check for understanding (3 min):** What makes media digital?

## Practice Activity
- Classification: Sort examples into 'related to Digital media' and 'not related'.
- Short response: Write two sentences using key vocabulary.
- Teacher: Provide concrete examples and model one classification.
- Students: Share answers with a partner.
- Practice: Characterize digital media.

## Assessment
- Observation during activity.
- Oral Q&A: Students explain one part of Digital media.
- Quick written check: 3 short questions.
- Mini rubric: 3 = accurate and complete, 2 = mostly correct, 1 = needs support

## Homework
- Write a short paragraph about Digital media using at least 4 vocabulary terms.
