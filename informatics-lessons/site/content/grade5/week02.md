# Grade 5 - Week 2: Transmitting information
## Learning Objectives
- Explain Transmitting information in clear, age-appropriate language.
- Identify the key parts or steps of Transmitting information.
- Apply Transmitting information to a real-life or classroom example.
- Demonstrate: Give examples of communication channels.
- Demonstrate: Sources and receivers of information.

## Assessment Criteria
- Uses correct terminology when explaining Transmitting information.
- Completes the practice activity accurately.
- Responds to oral questions with clear reasoning.
- Shows understanding of: give examples of communication channels, sources and receive.

## Lesson Timeline
- Introduction - 5 minutes
- Main Activity - 25 minutes
- Wrap-up - 10 minutes

## Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introduce Transmitting information and link it to everyday technology.
- Warm-up questions:
- - Where do we encounter this idea in daily life?
- - How does this help computers work correctly?
- Real-life examples:
- - A classroom example connected to Transmitting information.
- - Link to: give examples of communication channels, sources and receivers of information.
- Teacher: Briefly model the key idea using a quick sketch or object.
- Students: Share one example they know and explain why it fits.

## Main Content (25 minutes)
- Define Transmitting information in clear terms.
- Break down the key components or steps.
- Show a short, concrete example.
- Connect to prior knowledge from earlier lessons.
- Focus point: Give examples of communication channels.
- Focus point: Sources and receivers of information.
- Teacher: Model the process step-by-step and check for understanding.
- Students: Take brief notes and answer a quick concept check.

### Detailed Topic Study
- **Goal (2 min):** Explain how information moves from a source to a receiver.
- **Key ideas (8 min):** Communication includes a source, message, channel, and receiver. Channels can be spoken voice, paper, radio, or the internet. Noise can change the message.
- **Guided demo (6 min):** Telephone game: whisper a short message through 5 students, compare original vs final message.
- **Hands-on practice (6 min):** Students draw a simple communication diagram for sending a message to a friend.
- **Check for understanding (3 min):** Identify the source, channel, and receiver in a simple scenario.
